# Medical Emergency Response Drone (MERD)
### By The 4 Founders

MERD is a compact, low-cost autonomous quadcopter designed to 
deliver essential medical supplies — including blood units, 
vaccines, first-aid kits, and emergency medicines — to remote 
or traffic-congested regions of India.

## 🎯 Mission
To bridge the last-mile gap in emergency healthcare delivery 
using affordable drone technology, reducing response time to 
under 20 minutes.

## ✨ Key Features
- 📦 Payload capacity: 500g – 1kg
- 📡 GPS-guided autonomous navigation
- 🛡️ Obstacle avoidance sensors
- ❄️ Insulated delivery compartment
- 📱 SMS-based dispatch and real-time tracking
- 🔋 25–30 min flight time | 10–15 km range
- 💰 Total build cost under ₹30,000

## 🏥 Use Cases
- Accident sites and disaster zones
- Remote villages with limited road access
- Blood and plasma delivery between hospitals
- Emergency drug transport (epinephrine, antidotes)
- Vaccine delivery during outbreaks
- Hospital-to-hospital sample transfer
